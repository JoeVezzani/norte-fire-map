In a WARNING zone or nearby? Get ready now.

  • Pack the car and park it facing out. Keep the gas tank at least half full.
  • Put pets in one room so you can catch them fast.
  • Move flammable things away from the house: patio cushions, doormats, firewood, trash cans.
  • Close windows, doors and vents. Shut off the air conditioner's fresh-air intake.
  • Keep your phone charged and turn on emergency alerts. Sign up at AlertOC.
  • If you need extra time to leave (older adults, people with disabilities, families with babies, large animals), leave now. Do not wait for an order.

Smoke

  • Stay inside with windows and doors closed. Set your AC or HVAC to recirculate, or close its fresh-air intake. Use a MERV 13 filter if your system takes one.
  • Outside, wear a NIOSH-approved N95 that fits snugly. Paper dust masks and bandanas do not block smoke. Children under 2 should not wear masks.
  • In the car, windows up and air on recirculate. Skip outdoor exercise. Watch kids, older adults and anyone with asthma or heart disease.
  • Air quality by ZIP: AirNow Fire and Smoke Map or South Coast AQMD. Trouble breathing or chest pain: call 911.

Pets and large animals

  • Take your pets with you. Do not leave them behind or tied up. Bring a leash or carrier, food, water, medicine, vaccine records and a photo of your pet.
  • Pet-friendly shelter: call or check with the shelter before you go. Pet rules at the two shelters are not yet confirmed.
  • Horses and livestock: move them early. Trailers are slow on smoky, crowded roads. No large-animal shelter has been announced for this fire yet. Check OC Animal Care and @OCSheriff. In past fires the county has used the OC Fair & Event Center, but call before you go.
  • Animal owners who need help: OC Animal Care, (714) 935-6848.

Power and gas

  • SCE may cut power in high winds. Check your address on the SCE outage map. Treat every downed line as live. Stay far away and call 911.
  • Gas: your priority is getting out. You do not need to shut off your gas meter before leaving. If you smell gas, leave right away and call 911 from outside. If your gas is shut off, do not turn it back on yourself; only SoCalGas can restore it.
  • Turn off propane tanks if you can do it quickly. Generators go outside only, never in a garage.

Going home

  • Not yet. Evacuation orders are still in place. Do not try to get around roadblocks.
  • The Sheriff and OCFA will lift orders zone by zone. You will hear it through AlertOC, Genasys Protect, @OCFireAuthority and your city's website. This page will repost it, but official alerts come first.
  • When you go back, bring ID showing your address, plus N95 masks, gloves, boots and water. Watch for hot spots, ash pits and downed lines.

Want to help?

  • Do not drive to the fire area. Extra cars block fire engines and people trying to leave.
  • Do not drop off clothes or goods unless a shelter publicly asks for them. Cash to an established relief group is the most useful gift.
  • To volunteer, sign up through an established group. Do not show up on your own. Watch for fake fundraisers.
