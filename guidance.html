In an evacuation ORDER zone? Leave now.

Zones under order right now: {ZONES}. Take the 6 P's and go. You have about 10 minutes.

  1. People and pets. Pets in carriers or on leashes first. Bring their food, water and medicine.
  2. Prescriptions. Medicines, glasses, hearing aids, CPAP, chargers.
  3. Papers. ID, insurance cards, deeds, passports. A phone photo of each is fine.
  4. Phones. Phone, charger, battery pack.
  5. Personal needs. Clothes, water, a mask (N95 if you have one).
  6. Plastic. Wallet, cards, some cash.

Close all windows and doors but leave them unlocked. Leave a light on so crews can see your house in the smoke. Do not stop to pack anything else. If you are not sure whether you are in a zone and you see fire or heavy smoke, leave anyway. Need help leaving? Call 911.

Driving out

  • Do not head south. I-5 is closed in both directions south of San Clemente because of the Henderson Fire at Camp Pendleton.
  • Do not plan on I-5 near the fire. A hard closure was requested from San Juan Creek Road to Camino De Los Mares.
  • Do not take Avenida La Pata south. Closed southbound from Ortega Highway to Avenida Vista Hermosa.
  • Do not take Ortega Highway into the mountains unless officers tell you to.
  • Drive away from the smoke. Go where deputies and fire crews point you, even if your map app says otherwise. Roads close fast.
  • Headlights on. Windows up. Air on recirculate. Drive slowly in smoke. Live closures: Caltrans QuickMap.

Lleve a su familia y sus mascotas, sus medicinas, sus documentos (identificación, seguro), su teléfono y cargador, y dinero. Cierre ventanas y puertas. Deje una luz encendida.
